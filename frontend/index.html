Score Smoothing (Interpolation Regularization)
Score Smoothing (Chen, ICLR 2026) penalizes non-smoothness in the learned velocity field, causing the model to interpolate between training concepts rather than memorize exact training pairs.
  • Penalty weight — strength of the Laplacian smoothness regularizer (0 = disabled). Recommended: 0.001–0.01.
  • Kappa (κ) — smoothing window δ = κ√t. Larger κ = more smoothing at small timesteps. Paper default: 1.44.
  • MC smoothing — Monte-Carlo average velocity target over jittered latents (paper §3.1, Eq. 9).
  • MC samples — number of jitter samples for MC averaging.
